# IdeaNexus Landing Page: Complete Structure & Copy

## STRATEGIC FOUNDATION

### Product Brief
**Product Name:** IdeaNexus  
**Category:** B2B SaaS Innovation Management Platform  
**Core Function:** Systematic capture, evaluation, and implementation tracking of employee ideas

### Product Vision
Transform how SMBs manage innovation by providing enterprise-level idea management without enterprise complexity—making systematic innovation accessible and actionable for teams of 5-100 employees.

### Target Audience
**Primary:** South African SMB decision-makers (Operations Directors, Innovation Managers, CEOs, Department Heads)  
**Company Size:** 5-100 employees  
**Budget Threshold:** R50,000+ annual innovation investment  
**Industries:** Manufacturing, Professional Services, Technology, Retail

### Primary Goal
Generate qualified demo requests from SMBs with a target conversion rate of 5-8% (exceeding industry benchmark of 3-5%)

### Key Pain Points
1. Great ideas disappear into email threads and forgotten meetings
2. No systematic evaluation process (HIPPO decision-making)
3. Spreadsheet chaos nobody maintains
4. Employee frustration when suggestions go into "the void"
5. No visibility into innovation pipeline
6. Wasted resources on misaligned ideas
7. Fear of complex enterprise software

### Primary Value Proposition
"Systematic innovation management designed specifically for SMBs—giving you enterprise-level idea tracking without the complexity, cost, or consultants."

### Key Differentiators
1. **Goldilocks Positioning:** Neither too simple (just a spreadsheet) nor too complex (enterprise bloat)
2. **SMB-Native Design:** Built for 5-100 employees, not scaled-down enterprise
3. **Implementation Speed:** Up and running in 15-30 minutes
4. **No Training Required:** Intuitive interface teams actually use
5. **South African Focus:** POPIA-compliant, local support, SA-based servers
6. **Fair Evaluation Framework:** Objective criteria vs. politics
